# AETERNA Handoff Checklist

Version: 1.0

Status: Operational

---

Before creating a handoff:

□ Current objective identified.

□ Current implementation status described.

□ Completed work listed.

□ Remaining work listed.

□ Known issues documented.

□ Open questions documented.

□ Protocol constraints included.

□ Applicable canonical documents listed.

□ Recommendations clearly separated from completed work.

□ Assumptions explicitly identified.

□ Handoff reviewed for completeness.
